### Profit-Pilot highlights

- A modular workflow spanning data collection, feature engineering, environment design, training, evaluation, and reporting
- A custom Gymnasium-style multi-asset environment with realistic considerations such as fees, slippage, and risk constraints
- Reproducible notebooks, configuration, tests, documentation, and experiment artifacts
- Historical research results presented alongside benchmark comparisons

> **Note:** Profit-Pilot is an educational and research project. It is not financial advice or a recommendation to trade.
